# TikTok PH dance creator lead list

For Isaac / Cuzzi (`gitcuzzi`). Public-web research only. Nobody was messaged.

A manager can copy a handle, open one URL, and DM or email from the contact column. Every row is a real public profile that was opened. No invented handles.

**Checked:** 23 August 2026.

---

## How to use this list

1. Start with **Table A** (500–2,500 followers). Those are the accounts that match the brief.
2. If you need more people to contact today, use **Table B** (verified adult PH / Filipino dance people just outside the band) and **Table C** (larger dancers who already publish an email).
3. Prefer Instagram or email when listed. Otherwise DM the public TikTok.
4. Before paying anyone, open the TikTok and confirm recent **dance** posts. A few Table A rows match a known Manila dancer by unique name/handle but have a thin bio.

---

## How follower counts were verified

TikTok’s public web profile pages were fetched without logging in. The public HTML includes `followerCount`, `videoCount`, `heartCount`, and the bio (`signature`). Those numbers are **live public-page counts from 23 August 2026**, not estimates and not Social Blade.

TikTok rate-limits anonymous traffic. Some later refreshes returned an empty shell page. When that happened, the last successful public-page parse for that handle was kept and dated. Instagram itself redirected to login, so Instagram was used only when a handle was already published on a public website or in a TikTok bio.

Third-party directories (Optell, Toklytics, Nude Floor, Zero Studio, Unfold, 808 Studio, hyp.ed) were used to **find names**, not to invent follower counts.

---

## What this brief is hard

Indexed “PH TikTok dancer” lists are almost all 10k–millions (Niana, Austin Ong, A-Team, Femme MNL, G-Force stars). Adult working dancers in Manila often either barely use TikTok (under 400) or already grew past 8k.

Exact 1,000 is rare. The 500–2,500 band below is the real set we could verify. Table B exists so you still have 15+ named dance people to contact without opening 20 extra tabs.
